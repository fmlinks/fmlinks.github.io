MICCAI 2026 / THE METHOD

Covariance-guided.
Template-connected.

HeartVolMesh reconstructs a topology-consistent cardiac surface from CTA, then warps a fixed tetrahedral template while retaining its connectivity and vertex correspondence.

Why a volume mesh?

In-silico cardiac studies need an interior discretization as well as a surface. Thin walls and multi-structure junctions make faithful geometry and consistent connectivity especially valuable.

The core idea

Represent every template vertex as an anisotropic Gaussian kernel. Image-conditioned graph deformation predicts displacement and Cholesky-parameterized covariance, followed by template-driven volumetric registration.

Beyond isolated coordinates

Each vertex stores [x, y, z] plus six covariance parameters. The graph retains topology while learned anisotropy expresses spatially varying, direction-dependent geometric support.

Covariance-guided matching

The paper's surface-learning objective uses a covariance-aware negative log-likelihood with edge, Laplacian and normal regularization:

ℓ(x, y; Σ) = ½ (x − y)ᵀ Σ⁻¹ (x − y) + β log det Σ

This describes the paper's learning objective. The distributed agent skill uses frozen inference weights and does not train by default.

IMAGE FEATURES

Volume → graph

A 3D CNN extracts multi-scale features. Sampling at the current vertex positions brings image information into a GNN over the mesh graph.

SURFACE LEARNING

Coarse → fine

Progressive graph refinement predicts vertex displacements and Cholesky parameters for anisotropic covariance.

VOLUME PROPAGATION

Boundary → interior

The fitted surface motion is propagated to every tetrahedral-template vertex, preserving the fixed connectivity. The public pipeline uses one shared joint TPS field.

THE PAPER / COMPARISONS & EVALUATION

Geometry.
Measured and compared.

The original project page's scientific figures and reported cohort results are preserved below. These paper experiments are separate from the selected reconstruction and EP examples above.

Dataset & training

Multi-centre in-house data: 900 patients / 4,000 temporal instances. Patient-level split: 800 patients / 3,600 training instances and 100 patients / 400 validation instances.

Paper configuration: NVIDIA A100 80GB, Adam, 100 epochs, batch size 1, 128³ input crop and 300³ deformation-field resolution.

Evaluation protocol

Surface structures: LA, LV, RA, RV and LV myocardium. Volumetric boundaries: LA, LV, RA and RV myocardium.

Symmetric Chamfer distance (CD), HD95 and normal consistency (NC), plus inverted elements, minimum scaled Jacobian and minimum dihedral angle.

cd HeartVolMesh
python bootstrap.py --workspace ./workspace
python run_inference.py --workspace ./workspace \
  --image ./CTA.nii.gz --output ./result
